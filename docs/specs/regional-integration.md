# Regional Integration

## Mission 300 Regional Integration Dashboard Specification

## A. Headline Performance Cards

Energy, capacity and financial values remain in their reported units. Currency values are not combined without an approved conversion basis.

| Pillar or Scope | Metric or Dashboard Element | Visual Type | Target or Benchmark | Target Comparison and Progress Behaviour | Information Icon Content: Definition, Methodology and Data Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Regional Integration | Available cross-border interconnector capacity | KPI card in megawatts or megavolt-amperes | Approved interconnector and transmission-expansion plan; specific target to be confirmed | Compare with the previous period and approved plan where available. Retain the reported unit. | Definition: Available transfer capacity of Nigeria's cross-border interconnectors. Methodology: Use reported available capacity by interconnector. Do not combine megawatts and megavolt-amperes into one total. Data source: Transmission Company of Nigeria and Nigerian Independent System Operator. |
| Regional Integration | Electricity exported | KPI card showing national gigawatt-hours with country-pair breakdown | Approved regional integration strategy when available; no confirmed numeric export target | Compare with the previous period. Convert source megawatt-hours to gigawatt-hours by dividing by 1,000 for national totals. | Definition: Electricity exported through metered cross-border transactions. Methodology: Sum validated export energy by country pair and period. Preserve megawatt-hours at transaction level and use gigawatt-hours only for clearly labelled aggregates. Data source: Nigerian Independent System Operator, Transmission Company of Nigeria and Nigerian Electricity Regulatory Commission or Market Operator records. |
| Regional Integration | Cross-border revenue collected | KPI card in United States dollars or naira with explicit currency | Applicable invoiced amount; no separate Compact revenue target | Compare collected revenue with the previous period and current invoice. Do not aggregate currencies without an approved rate. | Definition: Cash collected from cross-border electricity buyers. Methodology: Sum validated receipts by buyer, period and currency. Data source: Nigerian Electricity Regulatory Commission cross-border payment tables and Market Operator settlement records. |
| Regional Integration | Cross-border payment performance | Progress card with buyer range | 100% of applicable cross-border payment obligation | Compare actual payment with applicable obligation and previous period. | Definition: Actual cross-border payments as a share of applicable obligations. Methodology: Actual payment divided by applicable payment obligation, multiplied by 100, using the same buyer, period and currency. Data source: Nigerian Electricity Regulatory Commission cross-border payment tables. |

## B. Cross-Border Energy Trade

| Pillar or Scope | Metric or Dashboard Element | Visual Type | Information Icon Content: Definition, Methodology and Data Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Regional trade | Electricity exports by country pair and period | Stacked columns with country-pair table | Definition: Metered electricity exported to each buyer or country pair. Methodology: Aggregate source megawatt-hours by country pair and period. Convert to gigawatt-hours only for national summaries. Data source: Nigerian Independent System Operator, Transmission Company of Nigeria and Market Operator records. |
| Regional trade | Trade volume and interconnector utilisation | Combined energy trend and utilisation table | Definition: Export energy and use of available interconnector capacity. Methodology: Calculate utilisation only where comparable transferred load and available capacity are provided for the same interconnector and period. Data source: Nigerian Independent System Operator and Transmission Company of Nigeria. |

## C. Cross-Border Invoices and Payments

| Pillar or Scope | Metric or Dashboard Element | Visual Type | Information Icon Content: Definition, Methodology and Data Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Regional finance | Invoice, obligation, actual payment and arrears by buyer | Buyer-level financial table | Definition: Cross-border billing and settlement position. Methodology: Arrears equal applicable obligation minus actual payment. Show United States dollars and naira in separate fields. Data source: Nigerian Electricity Regulatory Commission and Market Operator settlement records. |
| Regional finance | Payment performance by buyer | Ranked bars with quarterly trend | Definition: Buyer payment compliance. Methodology: Actual payment divided by applicable obligation, multiplied by 100, by buyer and period. Data source: Nigerian Electricity Regulatory Commission cross-border payment tables. |

## D. Interconnectors and Regional Market Reform

| Pillar or Scope | Metric or Dashboard Element | Visual Type | Information Icon Content: Definition, Methodology and Data Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Interconnection | Interconnector asset and project register | Map or table with megawatts, megavolt-amperes, kilovolts, kilometres and status | Definition: Installed and available interconnector capacity and associated line characteristics. Methodology: Keep capacity, voltage and line-length units as separate fields and record commissioning or project status. Data source: Transmission Company of Nigeria project and operational records. |
| Regional market | Grid synchronisation milestones | Stage tracker | Definition: Nigeria's technical and operational participation in the synchronised West African grid. Methodology: Track physical interconnection, tests, operational synchronisation and active market participation as separate stages with dates and evidence. Data source: Transmission Company of Nigeria and Nigerian Independent System Operator records. |
| Regional market | Harmonised transmission-pricing adoption | Milestone and tariff-reference table | Definition: Adoption and application of the approved regional transmission charging framework. Methodology: Record adoption status, effective date, tariff components and evidence. Keep United States dollars per megawatt-hour and other tariff units explicit. Data source: Nigerian Electricity Regulatory Commission, Transmission Company of Nigeria and approved regional framework records. |

## E. Delivery Status and Priority Bottlenecks

| Pillar or Scope | Metric or Dashboard Element | Visual Type | Information Icon Content: Definition, Methodology and Data Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Regional Integration | Regional trade and infrastructure delivery exceptions | Stacked status bar linked to ranked exceptions | Definition: Priority interconnector, settlement and market-integration records affected by delays. Data source: Implementation Register, Bottleneck Register and responsible-institution updates. |
