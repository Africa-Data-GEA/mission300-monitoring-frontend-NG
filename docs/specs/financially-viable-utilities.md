# Financially Viable Utilities

## Mission 300 Financially Viable Utilities Dashboard Specification

## A. Headline Performance Cards

Only the four headline cards show targets and comparison behaviour. Detailed monetary, energy and tariff measures remain in their source units.

| Pillar or Scope | Metric or Dashboard Element | Visual Type | Target or Benchmark | Target Comparison and Progress Behaviour | Information Icon Content: Definition, Methodology and Data Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Financially Viable Utilities | National metering rate | Progress card with metered and active-customer counts | 100% metering by 2027 | Compare with the previous period and applicable annual metering milestone. | Definition: Share of active registered customers with functioning meters. Methodology: Total metered active customers divided by total active registered customers, multiplied by 100. Data source: Nigerian Electricity Regulatory Commission Monthly Metering Factsheet and Quarterly Reports. |
| Financially Viable Utilities | Aggregate Technical, Commercial and Collection loss rate | KPI card with lower-is-better direction and Distribution Company range | Approved Multi-Year Tariff Order and Performance Improvement Plan benchmark for each Distribution Company; no single current national Compact target | Compare the official national weighted rate with the previous period and the relevant allowed-loss benchmarks. | Definition: Official Aggregate Technical, Commercial and Collection loss rate. Methodology: Use the Nigerian Electricity Regulatory Commission reported weighted national value. A system recalculation may validate the source but must not replace it. Data source: Nigerian Electricity Regulatory Commission Quarterly Reports. |
| Financially Viable Utilities | Combined market-remittance performance | Progress card with Nigerian Bulk Electricity Trading Plc and Market Operator components | 100% of the applicable combined remittance obligation | Compare with the previous period and 100% compliance. Keep both market components visible. | Definition: Actual combined remittance against the combined applicable obligation. Methodology: Total actual remittance to Nigerian Bulk Electricity Trading Plc and the Market Operator divided by the total applicable obligation to both, multiplied by 100. Calculate each component separately before aggregation. Data source: Nigerian Electricity Regulatory Commission market-remittance tables. |
| Financially Viable Utilities | Tariff shortfall | Fiscal exposure card in naira billions | Zero tariff shortfall by 2027 | Compare with the approved reduction path and previous comparable period. A decline is positive. | Definition: Approved fiscal shortfall arising from the difference between cost-reflective and allowed end-user tariffs. Methodology: Outstanding shortfall equals approved subsidy requirement minus funded or paid subsidy. Label approved, funded, paid and outstanding values separately. Data source: Nigerian Electricity Regulatory Commission and Federal Ministry of Finance records. |

## B. Metering

| Pillar or Scope | Metric or Dashboard Element | Visual Type | Information Icon Content: Definition, Methodology and Data Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Metering | Metered customers, unmetered customers and metering gap | National trend with Distribution Company comparison | Definition: Customer metering stock and the remaining unmetered customer gap. Methodology: Metering gap equals active registered customers minus metered customers. Metering-gap rate equals unmetered customers divided by active customers, multiplied by 100. Data source: Nigerian Electricity Regulatory Commission Monthly Metering Factsheet and Quarterly Reports. |
| Metering | Meter installations by delivery channel | Stacked columns for Meter Asset Provider, Meter Acquisition Fund, vendor-financed and Distribution Company-financed installations | Definition: Meters installed during the reporting period by financing or delivery arrangement. Methodology: Count validated installations by channel and period. Do not add cumulative and period values in the same total. Data source: Nigerian Electricity Regulatory Commission metering records. |
| Metering | Metering density by Distribution Company | Ranked horizontal bars | Definition: Metered active customers as a share of active registered customers for each Distribution Company. Methodology: Metered customers divided by active customers for the same Distribution Company and period, multiplied by 100. Data source: Nigerian Electricity Regulatory Commission Monthly Metering Factsheet. |

## C. Energy, Billing, Collection and Losses

Use megawatt-hours for detailed Distribution Company records and convert to gigawatt-hours only for clearly labelled aggregates.

| Pillar or Scope | Metric or Dashboard Element | Visual Type | Information Icon Content: Definition, Methodology and Data Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Utility performance | Energy received and energy billed | Grouped bars by Distribution Company in megawatt-hours | Definition: Energy delivered to and billed by each Distribution Company. Methodology: Use source megawatt-hours. Billing efficiency equals energy billed divided by energy received, multiplied by 100. Data source: Nigerian Electricity Regulatory Commission Quarterly Reports. |
| Utility performance | Revenue billed and revenue collected | Grouped bars by Distribution Company in naira billions | Definition: Customer revenue billed and actually collected. Methodology: Collection efficiency equals revenue collected divided by revenue billed, multiplied by 100. Data source: Nigerian Electricity Regulatory Commission Quarterly Reports. |
| Utility performance | Loss rate and variance from allowed loss | Comparison bars by Distribution Company | Definition: Actual Aggregate Technical, Commercial and Collection loss against the allowed Multi-Year Tariff Order loss. Methodology: Variance equals actual loss percentage minus allowed loss percentage, reported in percentage points. Data source: Nigerian Electricity Regulatory Commission Quarterly Reports and Multi-Year Tariff Order documents. |

## D. Market Invoices, Obligations and Remittances

Show Nigerian Bulk Electricity Trading Plc and Market Operator components separately before the combined market view.

| Pillar or Scope | Metric or Dashboard Element | Visual Type | Information Icon Content: Definition, Methodology and Data Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Nigerian Bulk Electricity Trading Plc | Invoice, Federal Government subsidy share, Distribution Company obligation, actual remittance and performance | Detailed table by Distribution Company with national totals | Definition: Nigerian Bulk Electricity Trading Plc market invoice and the applicable Distribution Company payment obligation. Methodology: Performance equals actual remittance divided by the applicable obligation, multiplied by 100. Do not use the gross invoice as denominator when a subsidy share changes the obligation. Data source: Nigerian Electricity Regulatory Commission market-remittance tables. |
| Market Operator | Invoice, Distribution Company obligation, actual remittance and performance | Detailed table by Distribution Company with national totals | Definition: Market Operator invoice and the applicable Distribution Company payment obligation. Methodology: Performance equals actual remittance to the Market Operator divided by the Market Operator obligation, multiplied by 100. Data source: Nigerian Electricity Regulatory Commission market-remittance tables. |
| Combined market | Combined invoice, obligation, actual remittance and performance | National trend and Distribution Company comparison | Definition: Combined Nigerian Bulk Electricity Trading Plc and Market Operator market position. Methodology: Sum comparable monetary components in naira billions, then divide combined actual remittance by combined obligation, multiplied by 100. Data source: Nigerian Electricity Regulatory Commission market-remittance tables. |

## E. Tariffs and Cost Reflectivity

| Pillar or Scope | Metric or Dashboard Element | Visual Type | Information Icon Content: Definition, Methodology and Data Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tariff | Service-Based Tariffs for Bands A to E | Tariff matrix by Distribution Company in naira per kilowatt-hour | Definition: Approved end-user tariff for each service band. Methodology: Display the approved rate, effective date and applicable Multi-Year Tariff Order. Do not average bands without customer weights. Data source: Nigerian Electricity Regulatory Commission Multi-Year Tariff Order tariff orders. |
| Tariff | Weighted average end-user tariff | Trend and Distribution Company comparison in naira per kilowatt-hour | Definition: Customer-weighted average approved tariff. Methodology: Sum each band tariff multiplied by its applicable customer population, then divide by total customers included. Data source: Nigerian Electricity Regulatory Commission tariff orders and customer-population records. |
| Tariff | Cost-reflective tariff and gap | Grouped bars and gap table in naira per kilowatt-hour and percentage | Definition: Difference between approved cost-reflective benchmark and weighted end-user tariff. Methodology: Gap equals cost-reflective tariff minus weighted average tariff. Gap rate equals the gap divided by the cost-reflective tariff, multiplied by 100. Data source: Nigerian Electricity Regulatory Commission tariff orders and cost-of-service determinations. |
| Tariff | Approved, funded, paid and outstanding tariff shortfall | Annual and year-to-date fiscal table in naira billions | Definition: Tariff-shortfall financing position. Methodology: Keep approved subsidy requirement, amount funded, amount paid and outstanding balance as separate fields. Data source: Nigerian Electricity Regulatory Commission and Federal Ministry of Finance. |

## F. Supply, Performance Plans and Reform Delivery

| Pillar or Scope | Metric or Dashboard Element | Visual Type | Information Icon Content: Definition, Methodology and Data Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Supply | Average daily supply by Service-Based Tariff band | Distribution Company heatmap in hours per day | Definition: Average daily electricity-supply hours by service band. Methodology: Average reported daily supply hours for the selected month and band. Band A is the primary view; Bands B to E appear where centrally available. Data source: Nigerian Electricity Regulatory Commission smart-meter and supply-hours records. |
| Performance Improvement Plans | Milestones due, achieved and achievement rate | Status table by utility | Definition: Delivery of approved utility Performance Improvement Plan milestones. Methodology: Achievement rate equals milestones achieved in the period divided by milestones due, multiplied by 100. Data source: Nigerian Electricity Regulatory Commission Performance Improvement Plan monitoring records. |
| Reform delivery | Disbursement-Linked Indicators due, achieved and verified | Programme status table | Definition: Disbursement-Linked Indicator delivery under relevant reform programmes. Methodology: Achievement rate equals verified indicators achieved divided by indicators due, multiplied by 100. Data source: Federal Ministry of Power and Nigerian Electricity Regulatory Commission programme trackers and verification records. |
