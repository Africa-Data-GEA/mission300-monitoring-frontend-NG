# Private-Sector Participation

## Mission 300 Private-Sector Participation Dashboard Specification

## A. Headline Performance Cards

Committed, mobilized, disbursed and deployed capital must remain distinct. United States dollars and naira must not be added without an approved conversion basis.

| Pillar or Scope | Metric or Dashboard Element | Visual Type | Target or Benchmark | Target Comparison and Progress Behaviour | Information Icon Content: Definition, Methodology and Data Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Private-Sector Participation | Validated private capital mobilized for last-mile access | Progress card in United States dollar millions or billions | USD 15.5 billion by 2030 | Compare cumulative eligible mobilized capital from the January 2025 tracking baseline with the target. | Definition: Eligible private capital mobilized for Mission 300 last-mile access. Methodology: Sum validated eligible private transactions from the approved baseline, exclude public finance and remove duplicates. Display committed, mobilized and deployed amounts separately. Data source: Federal Ministry of Power, Rural Electrification Agency, Nigerian Electricity Regulatory Commission, Federal Ministry of Finance and implementing institutions. |
| Private-Sector Participation | Private financing committed | KPI card with segment split and currency label | Applicable approved programme or financing commitment; no single cross-programme target | Compare with the previous period and approved programme commitments without mixing currencies. | Definition: Private financing contractually committed to eligible programmes or projects. Methodology: Sum signed eligible commitments by currency and financing type. Do not treat announced or pipeline amounts as committed. Data source: Rural Electrification Agency, Federal Ministry of Power, Federal Ministry of Finance and programme records. |
| Private-Sector Participation | Private financing disbursed or deployed | KPI card with committed-to-disbursed conversion ratio | Applicable approved programme disbursement plan; no single cross-programme target | Compare disbursed or deployed capital with committed capital and the previous period. | Definition: Eligible private financing released or deployed to projects. Methodology: Sum validated disbursements by currency. Conversion ratio equals disbursed amount divided by committed amount for the same financing perimeter, multiplied by 100. Data source: Programme financial records, Rural Electrification Agency, Federal Ministry of Power and Federal Ministry of Finance. |
| Private-Sector Participation | Mini-grid developers with signed grant agreements | KPI card with agreement and planned-site counts | Target number to be confirmed against the approved Distributed Access through Renewable Energy Scale-Up programme design | Compare with the previous period and approved programme pipeline when confirmed. | Definition: Unique private mini-grid developers with executed grant agreements. Methodology: Count unique developers with signed agreements and report planned sites or connections separately. Data source: Rural Electrification Agency grant-agreement tracker. |

## B. Investment Mobilization and Deployment

| Pillar or Scope | Metric or Dashboard Element | Visual Type | Information Icon Content: Definition, Methodology and Data Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Investment | Capital by status and segment | Stacked columns by committed, mobilized, disbursed and deployed status | Definition: Private capital across mini-grid, Solar Home System, distribution and other approved last-mile segments. Methodology: Classify each transaction once by the agreed status and segment. Keep public and private capital separate and show currencies separately. Data source: Federal Ministry of Finance, Federal Ministry of Power, Rural Electrification Agency and programme records. |
| Investment | Capital by financing type and source | Composition chart with transaction table | Definition: Eligible equity, debt, blended finance, grants linked to private mobilization and other approved instruments. Methodology: Aggregate validated transactions by financing type and source institution without double counting co-financed transactions. Data source: Federal Ministry of Finance and implementing-institution financing registers. |
| Investment | Financing conversion and undisbursed balance | Programme table | Definition: Movement from approved or committed financing to disbursement. Methodology: Undisbursed balance equals committed amount minus disbursed amount for the same programme, project, currency and reporting date. Data source: Programme financial records and responsible institutions. |

## D. Enabling Environment

## These supporting indicators appear when institutional data is available.

| Pillar or Scope | Metric or Dashboard Element | Visual Type | Information Icon Content: Definition, Methodology and Data Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Regulatory efficiency | Mini-grid permit processing time | Quarterly trend in calendar days | Definition: Elapsed calendar days from complete application to permit or registration decision. Methodology: Average decision date minus complete-application date for concluded applications. Exclude incomplete applications until completeness is established. Data source: Nigerian Electricity Regulatory Commission permit registry. |
| Tariff regulation | Distributed renewable energy tariff applications approved on time | Count and percentage trend | Definition: Applications decided within the applicable regulatory timeline. Methodology: Approved-on-time applications divided by complete applications decided in the period, multiplied by 100. Data source: Nigerian Electricity Regulatory Commission tariff-application records. |
| Finance infrastructure | Local-currency capital and project-preparation facility | Facility and instrument status table | Definition: Long-term local-currency financing and project-preparation support available to distributed renewable energy projects. Methodology: Show instrument or facility status, amount committed, amount disbursed and projects supported separately by currency. Data source: Rural Electrification Agency, Federal Ministry of Power and facility-management records. |
| Investor protection | Standardised contract framework | Milestone tracker | Definition: Approval and use of standardised distributed-renewable-energy contracts. Methodology: Record formal publication, adoption stage, effective date and evidence reference. Data source: Nigerian Electricity Regulatory Commission and Federal Ministry of Power official records. |
