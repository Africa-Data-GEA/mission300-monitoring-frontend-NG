## Mission 300 Clean Cooking Dashboard Specification, Provisional

## A. Headline Performance Cards

This page is provisional. Initial national access data should come from the Federal Ministry of Power and approved national sources; programme delivery data may come from the Rural Electrification Agency where held.

## B. National Access and Gap

| Pillar or Scope | Metric or Dashboard Element | Visual Type | Information Icon Content: Definition, Methodology and Data Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Clean-cooking access | Official access-rate trend | Annual line chart with source year | Definition: Official national clean-cooking access series. Methodology: Plot only published observations and retain source and reference year. Do not interpolate missing years unless the source publishes a modelled series. Data source: Federal Ministry of Power and approved national or Sustainable Development Goal 7 source. |
| Clean-cooking gap | Population without access | Annual gap trend | Definition: Population remaining without clean-cooking access. Methodology: Use the approved published series or the disclosed derivation from official population and access-rate values. Data source: Federal Ministry of Power and approved official sources. |
