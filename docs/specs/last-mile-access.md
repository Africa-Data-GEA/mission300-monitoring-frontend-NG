# Last-Mile Access

## Mission 300 Last-Mile Access Dashboard Specification

## A. Headline Performance Cards

Present access channels separately because grid, mini-grid and Solar Home System definitions are not directly additive without an approved deduplication method.

| Pillar or Scope | Metric or Dashboard Element | Visual Type | Target or Benchmark | Target Comparison and Progress Behaviour | Information Icon Content: Definition, Methodology and Data Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Last-Mile Access | Latest official national electricity access rate | KPI card with source year and publication date | Approved national access trajectory; the framework references a 9% annual improvement commitment, subject to confirmation of its application to the official series | Compare only with the previous official publication and approved trajectory. Do not interpolate monthly or quarterly values. | Definition: Official percentage of Nigeria's population with electricity access. Methodology: Use the latest approved national or Sustainable Development Goal 7 series. Do not construct an unofficial rate from customer and beneficiary counts. Data source: Federal Ministry of Power and approved Sustainable Development Goal 7 tracking source. |
| Last-Mile Access | New grid connections | KPI card showing reporting-period and annual cumulative connection counts | Annual grid-connection count target to be confirmed; do not convert the 9% access-rate commitment into a connection target | Show period change. Add a target marker only after an approved connection-count target is provided. | Definition: New direct grid connections added during the reporting period. Methodology: Current active registered customers minus comparable prior-period active registered customers, with adjustments disclosed where customer records are reclassified. Data source: Nigerian Electricity Regulatory Commission Quarterly Reports and Monthly Metering Factsheet. |
| Last-Mile Access | Active mini-grid household connections | Progress card with household and Micro, Small and Medium Enterprise values separated | 750,000 household connections under the Distributed Access through Renewable Energy Scale-Up programme by 2030 | Compare cumulative active household connections with the target and show the previous-quarter change. | Definition: Active customer connections served by operational mini-grids. Methodology: Sum validated active household connections. Report Micro, Small and Medium Enterprise connections separately and do not combine them with household connections without a clear label. Data source: Rural Electrification Agency programme and mini-grid registry records. |
| Last-Mile Access | Solar Home Systems deployed | Progress card showing reporting-period and cumulative systems | 2.75 million additional Solar Home Systems by 2030 | Compare eligible cumulative deployment from the approved baseline with the target. | Definition: Eligible standalone Solar Home Systems sold or deployed. Methodology: Count validated systems and distinguish reporting-period deployment from cumulative deployment. Data source: Rural Electrification Agency programme records. |

## B. Grid Access

| Pillar or Scope | Metric or Dashboard Element | Visual Type | Information Icon Content: Definition, Methodology and Data Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Grid access | Active registered grid customers | Quarterly national trend with Distribution Company drill-down | Definition: Cumulative active registered grid customers. Methodology: Sum active registered customers across all Distribution Companies for the selected period. Data source: Nigerian Electricity Regulatory Commission Monthly Metering Factsheet and Quarterly Reports. |
| Grid access | Grid connections by customer class | Stacked bars by residential, commercial and industrial class where available | Definition: New or active grid connections classified by customer type. Methodology: Use the source classification and avoid reallocating unclassified customers without evidence. Data source: Nigerian Electricity Regulatory Commission records. |

## C. Mini-Grid Delivery

| Pillar or Scope | Metric or Dashboard Element | Visual Type | Information Icon Content: Definition, Methodology and Data Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mini-grids | Operational mini-grids | National count with programme and site-status drill-down | Definition: Mini-grid systems commissioned, operational and serving customers. Methodology: Count unique operational site identifiers. Exclude planned, under-construction and non-operational sites from the operational total. Data source: Rural Electrification Agency mini-grid registry and programme records, with Nigerian Electricity Regulatory Commission permit records where applicable. |
| Mini-grids | Household, enterprise and total active connections | Grouped bars with separate connection types | Definition: Active connections served by operational mini-grids. Methodology: Sum household and Micro, Small and Medium Enterprise connections separately. A total may be shown only as their clearly labelled sum. Data source: Rural Electrification Agency programme records. |
| Mini-grids | Mini-grid pipeline and commissioning status | Stage funnel or status table | Definition: Approved or contracted mini-grid sites and planned connections not yet operational. Methodology: Count unique sites and planned connections by current stage. Do not include pipeline connections in delivered totals. Data source: Rural Electrification Agency grant-agreement, programme and project registers. |
| Mini-grids | Operational mini-grid capacity | Technology bars in kilowatts or megawatts with explicit unit | Definition: Installed capacity of commissioned operational mini-grids. Methodology: Sum site capacity in kilowatts and convert to megawatts by dividing by 1,000 for national totals. Data source: Rural Electrification Agency mini-grid registry. |

## D. Solar Home Systems and Productive Use

| Pillar or Scope | Metric or Dashboard Element | Visual Type | Information Icon Content: Definition, Methodology and Data Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Solar Home Systems | Systems deployed by period and programme | Columns for period deployment with cumulative line | Definition: Solar Home Systems sold or deployed through approved programmes. Methodology: Count validated units by reporting period and calculate cumulative units without double counting replacement systems. Data source: Rural Electrification Agency programme records. |
| Solar Home Systems | People reached | Beneficiary trend with system-count context | Definition: Estimated beneficiaries reached through Solar Home Systems. Methodology: Multiply eligible household systems by the approved household-size factor. Display the factor, source year and limitation in the information icon. Data source: Rural Electrification Agency records and the approved national household-size source. |
| Productive use | Enterprises connected and productive-use equipment | Grouped counts by programme and equipment type | Definition: Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises connected through distributed renewable energy and productive-use equipment deployed. Methodology: Count validated enterprise connections and equipment units separately by programme and type. Data source: Rural Electrification Agency programme records. |
