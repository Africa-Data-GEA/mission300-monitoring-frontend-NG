# State & DisCo Performance

## Mission 300 State and Distribution Company Performance Specification

## A. Headline Performance Cards

The default headline view is Distribution Company performance because the initial collection is national and centrally maintained. State values appear only where an approved source provides them.

| Pillar or Scope | Metric or Dashboard Element | Visual Type | Target or Benchmark | Target Comparison and Progress Behaviour | Information Icon Content: Definition, Methodology and Data Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Distribution Company performance | Metering rate | KPI card for selected Distribution Company or national weighted value | 100% by 2027 | Compare with the previous period and applicable metering milestone. | Definition: Metered active customers as a share of active registered customers. Methodology: Metered customers divided by active customers for the same Distribution Company and period, multiplied by 100. Data source: Nigerian Electricity Regulatory Commission Monthly Metering Factsheet. |
| Distribution Company performance | Aggregate Technical, Commercial and Collection loss rate | Lower-is-better KPI card | Applicable Multi-Year Tariff Order and Performance Improvement Plan benchmark | Compare with the previous period and allowed loss for the selected Distribution Company. | Definition: Official utility loss rate. Methodology: Use the reported Distribution Company value and show variance from allowed loss in percentage points. Data source: Nigerian Electricity Regulatory Commission Quarterly Reports and tariff orders. |
| Distribution Company performance | Collection efficiency | KPI card with revenue billed and collected | 100% | Compare with the previous period and 100% benchmark. | Definition: Share of billed customer revenue actually collected. Methodology: Revenue collected divided by revenue billed, multiplied by 100. Data source: Nigerian Electricity Regulatory Commission Quarterly Reports. |
| Distribution Company performance | Market-remittance performance | KPI card with market-component drill-down | 100% of applicable obligation | Compare with the previous period and 100% compliance. | Definition: Actual market remittance as a share of applicable obligation. Methodology: Actual remittance divided by the applicable Nigerian Bulk Electricity Trading Plc or Market Operator obligation, multiplied by 100. Data source: Nigerian Electricity Regulatory Commission market-remittance tables. |

## B. Distribution Company Comparison

Use megawatt-hours for detailed energy records and naira billions for revenue and market values.

| Pillar or Scope | Metric or Dashboard Element | Visual Type | Information Icon Content: Definition, Methodology and Data Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Customer and metering | Active, metered and unmetered customers | Ranked comparison table | Definition: Customer stock and metering position by Distribution Company. Methodology: Use the same reporting period for active and metered customers. Gap equals active minus metered. Data source: Nigerian Electricity Regulatory Commission metering records. |
| Energy and billing | Energy received, energy billed and billing efficiency | Grouped bars and comparison table | Definition: Energy received and billed by Distribution Company. Methodology: Use megawatt-hours. Billing efficiency equals energy billed divided by energy received, multiplied by 100. Data source: Nigerian Electricity Regulatory Commission Quarterly Reports. |
| Revenue and collection | Revenue billed, revenue collected and collection efficiency | Grouped bars in naira billions | Definition: Customer billing and cash collection by Distribution Company. Methodology: Collection efficiency equals revenue collected divided by revenue billed, multiplied by 100. Data source: Nigerian Electricity Regulatory Commission Quarterly Reports. |
| Market remittance | Invoice, obligation, actual remittance and performance | Comparison table with Nigerian Bulk Electricity Trading Plc and Market Operator tabs | Definition: Market settlement position by Distribution Company. Methodology: Calculate each market component against its applicable obligation before any combined value. Data source: Nigerian Electricity Regulatory Commission market-remittance tables. |

## C. Supply, Tariff and Delivery

| Pillar or Scope | Metric or Dashboard Element | Visual Type | Information Icon Content: Definition, Methodology and Data Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Supply | Average daily supply by service band | Heatmap in hours per day | Definition: Average daily supply hours by Distribution Company and tariff band. Methodology: Use reported monthly average hours. Keep Bands A to E separate. Data source: Nigerian Electricity Regulatory Commission supply-hours records. |
| Tariff | Service-Based Tariffs for Bands A to E | Distribution Company tariff matrix in naira per kilowatt-hour | Definition: Approved tariff by Distribution Company and service band. Methodology: Display approved rate and effective order; do not average without customer weights. Data source: Nigerian Electricity Regulatory Commission tariff orders. |
| Delivery | Performance Improvement Plan and project status | Utility delivery table | Definition: Utility plan milestones and linked projects. Methodology: Show milestones due, achieved, execution status, due dates, evidence and bottlenecks by utility. Data source: Nigerian Electricity Regulatory Commission Performance Improvement Plan monitoring and Implementation Register. |
| Governance | Reporting compliance and bottlenecks | Compliance matrix and exception list | Definition: Submission compliance and unresolved issues by Distribution Company. Methodology: Compare expected submissions with received approved submissions for each period and link open bottlenecks. Data source: Data Submission Register and Bottleneck Register. |

## D. State View

Do not infer state electricity-access rates from connection counts. Show only centrally maintained and validated state breakdowns.

| Pillar or Scope | Metric or Dashboard Element | Visual Type | Information Icon Content: Definition, Methodology and Data Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| State view | Access and distributed-energy delivery by state | Map or ranked table where data exists | Definition: State breakdown of mini-grid, Solar Home System, programme and project delivery. Methodology: Aggregate validated centrally maintained records by state. Do not request or fabricate new state or Local Government Area values for the initial collection. Data source: Rural Electrification Agency programme records and Implementation Register. |
| State view | State programme, project and bottleneck profile | State detail table | Definition: Delivery records linked to a selected state. Methodology: Filter approved implementation and bottleneck records by recorded project location. Data source: Implementation Register and Bottleneck Register. |
