# Generation & Network

## Mission 300 Generation and Network Dashboard Specification

## A. Headline Performance Cards

Only the four headline cards use targets and comparison behaviour. Lower sections provide diagnostic and delivery detail without repeating target columns.

| Pillar or Scope | Metric or Dashboard Element | Visual Type | Target or Benchmark | Target Comparison and Progress Behaviour | Information Icon Content: Definition, Methodology and Data Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Generation and Network | Available generation capacity | Key Performance Indicator (KPI) card showing available megawatts against installed megawatts with availability ratio and trend | Approved Integrated Resource Plan projection when available; no fixed current Compact target | Compare with the previous comparable period and add an approved plan marker when available. | Definition: Generation capacity available for dispatch, net of operating constraints. Methodology: Average daily available or dispatched capacity for the selected period. Availability ratio equals available capacity divided by installed nameplate capacity, multiplied by 100. Data source: Nigerian Electricity Regulatory Commission, Nigerian Independent System Operator and Transmission Company of Nigeria. |
| Generation and Network | Electricity generated and sent to the grid | KPI card showing national gigawatt-hours with trend sparkline | Approved Integrated Resource Plan demand projection when available; no fixed current Compact target | Compare with the previous comparable period and the approved demand projection when available. | Definition: Total electricity generated and sent out by grid-connected plants. Methodology: Aggregate consistent source records in gigawatt-hours. Convert megawatt-hours to gigawatt-hours by dividing by 1,000 and retain the reporting period. Data source: Nigerian Electricity Regulatory Commission Quarterly Reports, Nigerian Independent System Operator and Transmission Company of Nigeria. |
| Generation and Network | Renewable generation share | Progress card or horizontal bullet chart | 50% renewable energy share by 2030 | Show baseline, current share, expected trajectory for the selected year and the 2030 target. | Definition: Share of grid electricity generated from renewable sources. Methodology: Renewable generation in gigawatt-hours divided by total generation in gigawatt-hours, multiplied by 100. Renewable sources include hydro, solar and wind. Data source: Nigerian Electricity Regulatory Commission, Nigerian Independent System Operator and Transmission Company of Nigeria. |
| Generation and Network | Available transmission transfer capacity | KPI card showing reported megawatts or megavolt-amperes with trend and constraint indicator | Approved Transmission Company of Nigeria Performance Improvement Plan and Multi-Year Tariff Order capital-plan benchmark | Compare with the previous comparable period and the applicable approved plan. Retain the reported unit. | Definition: Available network capacity to evacuate generation and deliver it to distribution networks. Methodology: Use reported available transfer capacity. Calculate utilisation only where comparable transferred load and available capacity are both present. Data source: Transmission Company of Nigeria operational reports, Nigerian Independent System Operator dispatch records and Nigerian Electricity Regulatory Commission. |

## B. Generation Capacity and Output

Use two linked visuals to explain the national headline values by plant, technology and reporting period.

| Pillar or Scope | Metric or Dashboard Element | Visual Type | Information Icon Content: Definition, Methodology and Data Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Generation | Installed versus available capacity by plant and fuel | Clustered horizontal bars in megawatts | Definition: Installed capacity is grid-connected nameplate capacity; available capacity is capacity available for dispatch. Methodology: Sum installed megawatts and average available megawatts for the same assets and period. Show availability factor as available divided by installed, multiplied by 100. Data source: Nigerian Electricity Regulatory Commission Operational Factsheet and Quarterly Reports, Nigerian Independent System Operator and Transmission Company of Nigeria. |
| Generation | Generation output and mix by technology | Stacked columns in gigawatt-hours with technology share in the tooltip | Definition: Electricity sent to the grid by generation technology. Methodology: Aggregate national output in gigawatt-hours. Convert megawatt-hours by dividing by 1,000. Technology share equals technology output divided by total output, multiplied by 100. Data source: Nigerian Electricity Regulatory Commission generation tables, Nigerian Independent System Operator and Transmission Company of Nigeria. |

## C. System Reliability and Network Capacity

Show incident and network constraint detail without introducing unsupported reliability scores.

| Pillar or Scope | Metric or Dashboard Element | Visual Type | Information Icon Content: Definition, Methodology and Data Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| System reliability | System collapse incidents and restoration | Incident trend with compact incident table | Definition: Grid collapse events, impact and restoration performance. Methodology: Count incidents by period and show duration in hours, megawatts lost and restoration time for each event. Do not combine megawatts lost with energy values. Data source: Nigerian Electricity Regulatory Commission Quarterly Reports and Nigerian Independent System Operator or Transmission Company of Nigeria system records. |
| Transmission | Transfer capacity by corridor or substation | Ranked horizontal bars with capacity, utilisation where calculable and constraint flag | Definition: Available transfer capacity by corridor or substation, net of outages and constraints. Methodology: Use the reported megawatts or megavolt-amperes. Utilisation equals transferred load divided by comparable available capacity, multiplied by 100, only where both values exist. Data source: Transmission Company of Nigeria operational and substation reports, Nigerian Independent System Operator and Nigerian Electricity Regulatory Commission. |

## D. Network Expansion

## Keep different engineering units separate.

| Pillar or Scope | Metric or Dashboard Element | Visual Type | Information Icon Content: Definition, Methodology and Data Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Transmission | Transmission network expansion | Two-measure progress panel for line kilometres and substation megavolt-amperes | Definition: New transmission lines and substation capacity completed, energised and commissioned. Methodology: Sum certified line kilometres and certified substation megavolt-amperes separately for the selected period. Data source: Transmission Company of Nigeria commissioning reports, Nigerian Electricity Regulatory Commission Performance Improvement Plan monitoring and Federal Ministry of Power project records. |
| Distribution | Distribution network expansion | Progress table for feeder kilometres, transformer megavolt-amperes and transformer count | Definition: New or rehabilitated feeders and transformer assets commissioned. Methodology: Aggregate validated feeder kilometres, transformer capacity and transformer counts separately. National totals may drill down by Distribution Company where centrally maintained. Data source: Nigerian Electricity Regulatory Commission Performance Improvement Plan and utility-performance monitoring records. |

## E. Policy, Planning and Market Reform

## Use one reform tracker rather than separate cards for each milestone.

| Pillar or Scope | Metric or Dashboard Element | Visual Type | Information Icon Content: Definition, Methodology and Data Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Policy and planning | Power-sector planning reform milestones | Milestone tracker for the National Integrated Electricity Policy and Strategic Implementation Plan, least-cost plan, Integrated Resource Plan and competitive procurement framework | Definition: Status of the four Pillar 1 policy, planning and procurement reforms. Methodology: Apply the approved completion evidence for each milestone and record stage, date, responsible institution, evidence and next action. Data source: Federal Ministry of Power and Nigerian Electricity Regulatory Commission official policies, orders, gazettes and implementation records. |
| Market transition | Energy traded under bilateral contracts | Quarterly line chart with transaction volume and share | Definition: Share of market energy transacted under approved bilateral contracts. Methodology: Bilateral energy in megawatt-hours divided by total market energy in megawatt-hours, multiplied by 100. Use the same unit in numerator and denominator. Data source: Market Operator settlement records and Nigerian Electricity Regulatory Commission bilateral-contract registry. |

## F. Delivery Status and Priority Bottlenecks

## Use one linked split panel.

| Pillar or Scope | Metric or Dashboard Element | Visual Type | Information Icon Content: Definition, Methodology and Data Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Generation and Network | Priority programme, project and action delivery with bottlenecks | Stacked status bar linked to a ranked five-record exception table | Definition: Delivery status of priority Generation and Network records and their unresolved bottlenecks. Methodology: Count approved records by status and rank delayed, blocked, high and critical exceptions by severity, impact and days unresolved. Data source: Compact Progress Report, Implementation Register, Bottleneck Register and validated institutional updates. |
