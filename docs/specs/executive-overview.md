# Executive Overview

## Mission 300 Executive Overview Dashboard Specification

## A. Headline Cards

Only four cards appear at the top. Targets or benchmarks are displayed within the cards where applicable.

| Pillar or Scope | Metric or Dashboard Element | Visual Type | Target or Benchmark | Target Comparison and Progress Behaviour | Information Icon Content: Definition, Methodology and Data Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Cross-cutting | Priority milestones delivered on time | Wide progress card with Completed, On Track, Delayed and Blocked counts | 100% of due priority milestones completed on time | Progress bar to 100%. Show the current period against the previous comparable period. Use a green upward arrow when on-time delivery improves and a red downward arrow when it falls. | Definition and display notes: Definition of a priority milestone; calculation method; reporting period; records included; source; last updated date. Clarify that this is an implementation-delivery measure, not an average of all Key Performance Indicators.<br>Methodology and data source: Priority milestones completed by their due dates divided by priority milestones due by the selected reporting date, multiplied by 100. Source: approved Compact Progress Report, Implementation Register and updates from responsible institutions. |
| Last-Mile Access | Latest official national electricity access rate | Key Performance Indicator (KPI) card with source year prominently displayed | Approved national access trajectory. The current framework references a 9% annual improvement commitment, subject to confirmation of its application to the access-rate series. | Show the current official rate, the previous official publication and the approved target path. Use a green upward arrow when the official rate improves. Keep the source year visible at all times. | Definition and display notes: Definition; reference year; publication date; survey or modelling methodology; source; reporting frequency; rural and urban coverage where available.<br>Methodology and data source: Use only the latest officially published national electricity-access rate. Do not extrapolate the figure monthly or quarterly. Source: official Sustainable Development Goal 7 tracking publication and approved national source. |
| Generation and Network | Available generation capacity | KPI card showing available megawatts against installed megawatts, with trend sparkline | Compare with the approved Integrated Resource Plan projection when available. No single current Compact target. | Show variance from the previous comparable period. Use a green upward arrow when available capacity increases. Add a benchmark marker when an approved Integrated Resource Plan target becomes available. | Definition and display notes: Difference between installed capacity, available capacity, actual generation and transmission capacity; unit; reporting period; source; previous-period value; last updated date.<br>Methodology and data source: Average daily available or dispatched capacity during the selected reporting period. Availability ratio equals available capacity divided by installed nameplate capacity, multiplied by 100. Source: Nigerian Independent System Operator, Transmission Company of Nigeria and Nigerian Electricity Regulatory Commission. |
| Cross-cutting | Critical escalations requiring leadership attention | Alert card showing unresolved critical issues and overdue decisions | Zero unresolved critical escalations and zero overdue leadership decisions | Show the current count and days unresolved. Use a green downward arrow when unresolved critical escalations decline and a red upward arrow when they increase. | Definition and display notes: Severity definition; affected pillar; responsible institution; decision required; date raised; due date; days unresolved; recommended next action.<br>Methodology and data source: Count unresolved critical bottlenecks, blocked priority actions and leadership decisions whose due dates have passed. Source: Implementation Register and Bottleneck Register. |

Dynamic pillar behaviour: All Pillars shows the four default headline cards. When a pillar is selected, the delivery card recalculates for that pillar, the middle two cards change to the most important metrics for the selected pillar, and the escalation card filters to that pillar.

## B. Access Delivery by Channel

| Pillar or Scope | Metric or Dashboard Element | Visual Type | Target or Benchmark | Target Comparison and Progress Behaviour | Information Icon Content: Definition, Methodology and Data Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Last-Mile Access | New grid connections | Horizontal progress bar with annual trend | Annual grid-connection target to be confirmed. Do not convert the 9% access-rate commitment directly into a connection-count target without an approved conversion methodology. | Until an annual connection target is approved, show period-on-period trend only. Once approved, add a progress bar showing actual connections against the period target. Use a green upward arrow when new grid connections increase. | Definition and display notes: Definition of a grid connection; reporting period; current and prior active customers; customer-data adjustment caveat; source; last updated date.<br>Methodology and data source: Current active registered grid customers minus comparable prior-period active registered customers. A grid connection is a direct customer connection to a Distribution Company network and is not a mini-grid connection. Source: Nigerian Electricity Regulatory Commission Quarterly Reports and Monthly Metering Factsheet. |
| Last-Mile Access | Active mini-grid household connections | Horizontal progress bar against target | 750,000 household connections under the Distributed Access through Renewable Energy Scale-up programme by 2030 | Progress bar equals current cumulative active household connections divided by 750,000. Show the change from the previous quarter with a green upward arrow when connections increase. | Definition and display notes: Definition of an active mini-grid connection; household and business distinction; programme coverage; current value; target; reporting period; source.<br>Methodology and data source: Cumulative active household connections served by operational mini-grids. Business connections are reported separately and must not be added to household connections without a clear label. Source: Rural Electrification Agency programme records. |
| Last-Mile Access | Solar Home Systems deployed | Horizontal progress bar against target | 2.75 million additional Solar Home Systems by 2030 | Progress bar equals eligible additional systems deployed since the approved baseline divided by 2.75 million. Use a green upward arrow when cumulative eligible deployment increases. | Definition and display notes: Definition of a Solar Home System; baseline date; reporting-period versus cumulative deployment; system categories where available; target; source.<br>Methodology and data source: Cumulative eligible Solar Home Systems deployed through approved programmes. Reporting-period deployment and cumulative deployment must be distinguishable. Source: Rural Electrification Agency programme records and approved national reports. |

## C. National Performance and Target Progress

Use horizontal bullet charts. Each metric has its own scale, baseline, current value, expected value for the selected period and final target or approved benchmark.

| Pillar or Scope | Metric or Dashboard Element | Visual Type | Target or Benchmark | Target Comparison and Progress Behaviour | Information Icon Content: Definition, Methodology and Data Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Generation and Network | Renewable generation share | Horizontal bullet chart | 50% by 2030 | Show baseline, current share, expected trajectory for the selected year and the 50% target. Use a green upward arrow when the share increases. The chart must retain the reporting period because renewable output is seasonal. | Definition and display notes: Renewable technologies included; numerator; denominator; formula; baseline; current period; seasonal caveat; target; source.<br>Methodology and data source: Renewable electricity generated divided by total electricity generated, multiplied by 100. Renewable sources include hydro, solar and wind. Source: Nigerian Independent System Operator, Transmission Company of Nigeria and Nigerian Electricity Regulatory Commission generation records. |
| Financially Viable Utilities | National metering rate | Horizontal bullet chart | 100% by 2027 | Show current metering rate against the 100% target and applicable annual milestone. Use a green upward arrow when the rate rises and a red downward arrow when it falls. | Definition and display notes: Metered customers; active customers; formula; Compact baseline; current value; target; reporting date; source.<br>Methodology and data source: Total metered active customers divided by total active registered customers, multiplied by 100. Source: Nigerian Electricity Regulatory Commission Monthly Metering Factsheet and Quarterly Reports. |
| Financially Viable Utilities | Aggregate Technical, Commercial and Collection loss rate | Horizontal bullet chart with lower-is-better direction | Compare with approved Multi-Year Tariff Order and Distribution Company Performance Improvement Plan benchmarks. No single national Compact target is currently stated. | Show current loss rate, previous-period rate and applicable benchmark. Use a green downward arrow when losses decline and a red upward arrow when losses rise. | Definition and display notes: Definition; direction of improvement; current and previous periods; national weighted rate; best and worst Distribution Companies; applicable benchmark; source.<br>Methodology and data source: Display the official Nigerian Electricity Regulatory Commission weighted national Aggregate Technical, Commercial and Collection loss rate. System recalculation may support quality checking but should not replace the official reported value. Source: Nigerian Electricity Regulatory Commission Quarterly Reports. |
| Financially Viable Utilities | Market-remittance performance | Horizontal bullet chart | 100% remittance-obligation compliance | Show current performance against the 100% target. Use a green upward arrow when performance improves. Nigerian Bulk Electricity Trading Plc and Market Operator comparisons should be available in the tooltip or drill-down. | Definition and display notes: Invoice; subsidy share; applicable obligation; actual remittance; formula; current and previous period; target; source.<br>Methodology and data source: Actual remittance divided by the applicable remittance obligation, multiplied by 100. Calculate Nigerian Bulk Electricity Trading Plc and Market Operator values separately before aggregation. Source: Nigerian Electricity Regulatory Commission market-remittance tables. |
| Financially Viable Utilities | Tariff shortfall | Horizontal bullet chart with reduction trajectory | Zero tariff shortfall by 2027 | Show the reduction from the approved baseline toward zero and the expected value for the selected year. Use a green downward arrow when the shortfall decreases and a red upward arrow when it increases. | Definition and display notes: Approved, funded, paid and outstanding amounts; currency; baseline; current period; target; calculation method; source.<br>Methodology and data source: Display the approved annual or year-to-date tariff shortfall. Outstanding shortfall equals approved subsidy requirement minus subsidy funded or paid. Source: Nigerian Electricity Regulatory Commission and Federal Ministry of Finance. |
| Private-Sector Participation | Private capital mobilized | Horizontal bullet chart against target | USD 15.5 billion by 2030 | Progress bar equals cumulative eligible private capital mobilized divided by USD 15.5 billion. Show the expected trajectory for the selected year and use a green upward arrow when eligible capital increases. | Definition and display notes: Definitions of committed, mobilized and deployed capital; eligible sectors; tracking start date; current value; target; source institutions.<br>Methodology and data source: Cumulative eligible private capital mobilized for last-mile access from the January 2025 Mission 300 tracking baseline. Exclude public funding and duplicate transactions. Source: Federal Ministry of Power, Rural Electrification Agency, Nigerian Electricity Regulatory Commission, Federal Ministry of Finance and implementing institutions. |

## D. Delivery Status, Bottlenecks and Decisions

Use one linked split panel. A View All action opens the Bottlenecks and Exceptions module.

| Pillar or Scope | Metric or Dashboard Element | Visual Type | Target or Benchmark | Target Comparison and Progress Behaviour | Information Icon Content: Definition, Methodology and Data Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| All Compact pillars | Pillar delivery status and priority exceptions | Split panel with horizontal stacked bars by pillar and a ranked exception table showing a maximum of five records | All priority actions and milestones completed or on track; zero blocked priority records, overdue critical decisions and unresolved critical bottlenecks | Compare delayed, blocked and unresolved high or critical records with the previous reporting period. Use a green downward arrow when exceptions decline and a red upward arrow when they increase. | Definition: Consolidated priority delivery status by pillar linked to unresolved bottlenecks and overdue decisions. Methodology: Count approved priority records by Completed, On Track, At Risk, Delayed and Blocked status. Rank exceptions by severity, delivery impact and days unresolved. Do not average unrelated performance percentages. Data source: Compact Progress Report, Implementation Register and Bottleneck Register. |
